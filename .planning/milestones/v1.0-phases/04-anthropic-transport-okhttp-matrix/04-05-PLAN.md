<objective>
Retry transient failures exactly once, below the `AiProvider` seam, with an HTTP-level budget of at most three requests per logical call, and prove through the real pipeline that a retried call never executes a tool or commits an action twice (PROV-09, D-05, SC3). Give apps an ids-only window onto each attempt (orchestrator decision 1).

Purpose: A16 retry semantics: a retry re-sends HTTP, never re-runs app side effects; the one-shot body from 04-02 guarantees OkHttp does not add replays of its own.
Output: `RetryPolicy.kt`, the transport loop, the public observer types, three test classes.
</objective>
