<objective>
Make the ladder policy-aware and never-throw: `TierSelector` (`Linear`, `Fixed`), the per-execute `TierPolicy` pre-check over static `StrategyCapabilities` declarations (including the offline and ON_DEVICE rules), and the never-throw collapse with engine deadline and cancellation semantics, all through the single `guarded` helper.

Purpose: CORE-02..05 are the pipeline's contract with every consumer: a command either lands on the right tier or fails loudly and specifically, and nothing ever escapes `execute()` except real cancellation. Decisions: D-03 (timeouts become TIMEOUT), D-17 (Fixed misconfiguration at build), D-18 (pre-check with static declarations, internal ON_DEVICE hook).

Planner-resolved assumptions applied here (full list in 02-01-PLAN.md): research O3 (CORE-05 reading), research A5 (a throwing TierPolicySource becomes Failed(PolicyUnavailable) with zero executions), items 15 (unknown Fixed tier throws at build), 17 (no default engine deadline), 18 (unknown maxTier is a loud NoEligibleTier) and 19 (an unavailable ON_DEVICE-only tier fails without climbing).

Output: 3 new main files, 4 edited main files, 1 edited fixture, 2 new and 3 extended test classes.
</objective>
