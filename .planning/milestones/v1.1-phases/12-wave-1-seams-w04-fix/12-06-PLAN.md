<objective>
Carry the provider's tool-call id onto what the app sees (SEAM-06): `Extraction.callId` for the resolver/executor and
`ExecutedAction.providerCallId` on every recorded action, null for zero-call tiers. Per D-04 (and the orchestrator-accepted
brief correction) the id travels through internal plumbing only: an internal `submit(step, providerCallId)` overload on
`CommandSession`/`RunSession`, a `CommitCoordinator` overload, `ActionDetails.providerCallId`, and an internal
`HeldProposal` field so `commitHeld` keeps the id. The public `submit(step)` stays and records null. `heldRunId` and other
extras are out of scope.

Depends on 12-02 because both edit `SingleShotStrategy.kt` (12-02 adds the reasoning knob and onFailed; this plan changes
resolve/submitAll). Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 12 success criterion 2 (call-id clause and the 2-argument Extraction
constructor clause); SB 178 run-undo and Phase 17 build on it.
Output: two public read-only properties (additive), one public constructor overload, internal plumbing, tests.
</objective>
