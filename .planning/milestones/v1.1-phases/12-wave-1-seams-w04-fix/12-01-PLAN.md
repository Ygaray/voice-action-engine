all, so direct ids of those families also refuse pre-call (`toolsOnChat` false), the same mechanism the Astra pair
already uses.

## Edge-probe assumptions (spec-less fallback)

The deterministic edge probe returned three PROV-14 items and one PROV-15 item, all unresolved. Resolution here:
- PROV-14 adjacency ("when two ids just touch"): authored as a truth: `gpt-6-astral` and `gpt-6-astra` are different models; anchored regexes keep the adjacent id on `none`.
- PROV-14 empty ("empty/null input"): authored as a truth: a blank or unknown id keeps today's default rules.

- SEAM-03 (unclassified): flagged assumption, reviewed manually: exact-id match only (dated, suffixed and differently cased ids are unknown); covered in Task 3.

Flagged assumptions carried from research (not re-fetched individually): A1 forced tool choice works on `claude-sonnet-5`
(absence from Anthropic's rejection list, plus the brief's live 4/4); A3 `gpt-5.4-pro`, `gpt-5.2-pro`, `gpt-5.2-codex`,
`gpt-5-pro` behave like the re-fetched `gpt-5.5-pro`/`gpt-5.3-codex`; A4 routed `-pro`/`-codex` ids are left on today's
rules because the router's behavior is unverified (this plan applies the exclusion to direct OpenAI only).
