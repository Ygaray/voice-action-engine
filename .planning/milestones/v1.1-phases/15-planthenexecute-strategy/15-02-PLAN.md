<objective>
Make a later plan step use an earlier step's real write output (ROADMAP SC2), validate the whole plan before step 1
(D-02), and turn a plan that needs a lookup into a zero-side-effect escalation (the lookup half of PLAN-03). This is
the D-04 binding contract: the `$<stepId>.<key>` syntax is human-locked and one-way; this plan freezes its mechanics.

Purpose: write-output binding is the reason PlanThenExecute exists (SB 177). The mechanics written here are frozen at
the v1.1.0 tag and validated live by plan 15-07.
Output: `PlanBinding.kt`, full `PlanParse.kt` validation with the lookup verdict, three plan trace codes, binding in
`PlanRun`, and the binding, parse and lookup tests; API.md binding, lookup and telemetry sentences.

Orchestrator note (OI-2, recorded by plan 15-06's surface review): the grammar detail set below (letter-leading ids,
key = non-whitespace remainder, an undeclared reference is a rejection rather than a literal, no escape syntax in
v1.1) refines mechanics inside D-04 without changing the syntax. ACCEPTED as is by the orchestrator on 2026-10-06
(the same relay as RT-01; see 15-CONTEXT.md "## Runtime Decisions"), so implement it exactly as written; nothing is
pending on it.
</objective>
