<objective>
Pin the hold semantics (D-07 as amended by RT-01, SB condition #3), expose the ids of the steps a stopped plan never
ran (RT-01 point 4), and prove PLAN-04 with a Plan-specific test: once any step has committed, nothing escalates to a
later tier; a failure ends as a suppressed partial Completed and a hold ends as a terminal partial Completed.

Purpose: ROADMAP SC4 and the "held step reported held" half of SC1; SB 177's needs-confirmation sheet relies on the
held proposal surfacing while the earlier commits stay, and on knowing which steps never ran.
Output: the internal outcome channel and the public `CommandOutcome.Completed.remainingStepIds`, the RT-01 hold
mapping in the strategy, `RemainingStepIdsTest`, `PlanThenExecuteHoldTest`, `PlanThenExecuteSuppressionTest`, and
API.md sentences on holds, partial outcomes and the new field.

Deviation / ruling RT-01 (orchestrator 3b binding ruling via master relay, 2026-10-06; SB 177 objection to OI-1
adopted; recorded verbatim in 15-CONTEXT.md "## Runtime Decisions"): this plan was first written with the OI-1
last-step carve-out (a last-step hold ended Completed(null), not partial; a hold with steps remaining escalated and
TierWalk suppressed it). That carve-out is withdrawn. Now: (1) a hold with nothing committed returns
Escalate(Other("plan_step_held")), unchanged, and TierWalk suppresses it; (2) a hold after at least one committed step
returns a terminal Completed with partial = true that keeps the commits and outcome.held, and never escalates, because
a later tier would re-run the committed steps; (3) a last-step hold is case 2, or case 1 when nothing committed; (4) the
new public `CommandOutcome.Completed.remainingStepIds` lists the steps that never ran. Consequences here: a new Task 1
(the outcome channel and the public field), the hold mapping keyed on the run's committed steps instead of a last-step
flag, a single-step held plan now ending partial with a suppressed escalation (case 1) instead of the clean
SingleShot-like Completed, and the suppression test's hold case asserting a direct Completed instead of
escalation_suppressed. The field's name, shape and fill rule are the "[hold] RT-01 mechanics" runtime line in
15-CONTEXT.md; plan 15-06 records OI-1 as resolved and reviews the field as a +-only surface addition.
</objective>
