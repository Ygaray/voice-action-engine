Phase 9 AgenticLoop limits evidence - plan 09-09, Task 1 (limits precondition for the v1.0.0 cut)

Obligation: the AgenticLoop obeys the contract limits 6 iterations / 60000-token ceiling / 4096 per-turn tokens, read from
session.policy and session.tokensUsed (TierPolicy), never from a literal inside the loop.
  - 6 iterations (TierPolicy.maxIterations): the final permitted iteration never dispatches a tool, except a turn whose first call is terminal.
  - 60000-token ceiling (TierPolicy.tokenCeiling): pre-call refusal at or over the ceiling; a tool turn strictly over the ceiling fails before dispatch.
  - 4096 per-turn tokens (TierPolicy.maxTokensPerTurn): sent unchanged as request.maxTokens on every request of the run.
