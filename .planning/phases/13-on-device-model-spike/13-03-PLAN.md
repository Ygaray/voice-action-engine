and the host filter, the scoring math D-07 needs (Wilson lower bound, nearest-rank percentiles, a schema subset validator),
the per-envelope verdict rules over the locked thresholds, and a script that recomputes the verdict from committed evidence.

thresholds locked before the run; unmeasured must mean red.

`scripts/spike-evidence-filter.sh`, `scripts/verify-spike-evidence-filter.sh`.
