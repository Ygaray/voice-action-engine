<objective>
Make "never guesses" (GRAM-03, SC-3) a tested property instead of a hope: report ambiguity with its code, refuse at
build any pack whose rules overlap with different results, apply the full D-10 language-label table with
`tryOtherLanguage` and cross-pack agreement, add the derived input-length cap, and pin the behavior with a near-miss
corpus, a positive corpus and a mutation property.

Purpose: a grammar match writes with no model in the loop, so every convenience that raises the hit rate is a potential
silent wrong write (Research "Key insight"). This plan closes those doors.
Output: ambiguity self-check, label/agreement logic, `Builder.tryOtherLanguage`, the cap, and four test classes.
</objective>
