<objective>
Expand the tracer's plain-word matching into the real phrasing layer: the D-11 text fold and tokenizer, the D-03
template mini-syntax (literals, `[optional]`, `(a|b)`, `{slot}`, `<rule>`), an anchored matcher that enumerates every
parse and refuses on a second distinct result, per-language fillers, and the template half of build-time validation.

Purpose: after this plan an app can declare natural EN and ES phrasings with optional words, alternatives and
sub-rules, and its users' transcripts match regardless of case, accents, punctuation and hyphenation, while anything
outside the declared phrasings (an extra clause included) still goes to the next tier. Typed slots come in 14-05.
Output: `GrammarText.kt`, `TemplateParser.kt`, `TemplateExpander.kt`, `RuleMatcher.kt`, `PackValidator.kt`, the new
builder methods, and four test classes.
</objective>
