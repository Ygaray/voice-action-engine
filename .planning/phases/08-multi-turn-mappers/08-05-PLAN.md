<objective>
Stand up the one conformance suite (D-09, XCR-01) and prove it on the Anthropic mapper first, using two derived conversation goldens:
- four parallel calls, including a zero-argument call and an error result;
- thinking, redacted thinking and interleaved thinking over three turns.

The suite replays each golden conversation through the production encoder and decoder and checks four things: every turn's exact messages array, per-dialect tool-result encoding, byte-for-byte replay of every assistant turn across the whole history (XCR-02, D-02), and, through the real provider against MockWebServer, the fail-loud stamp and coverage behaviour 08-01 built (D-03, D-04).

Purpose: SC1 and SC2 need one suite that runs on both mappers. 08-06 binds it to Chat Completions without changing a single assertion.
Output: three new test Kotlin files, two derived fixtures, two manifest rows.

Contention: wave 5 is this plan only. It starts after 08-04 has merged and never runs two Gradle invocations at once.
</objective>
