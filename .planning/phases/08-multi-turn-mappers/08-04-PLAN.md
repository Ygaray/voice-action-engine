<objective>
Build the test-side infrastructure the conversation goldens need, before any golden exists:
- a strict manifest and loader for `golden/conversations`, plus a canonical printer (D-02) and a tree-aware hygiene scan;
- a sanitizer that renames ids consistently across every turn of one conversation and never touches thinking or reasoning (D-08);
- the one synthetic script that derived fixtures, the conformance suite and the live recorder all share (D-07 refreshed).

The existing Phase 5 sanitizer cannot be reused for ids. It numbers tool-call ids per body (turn 1 and turn 2 would both get `call_GOLDEN1`) and it scrubs inside thinking text. So it stays untouched, and only its regex constants are imported.

Purpose: 08-05 and 08-06 add derived fixtures that must pass these checks, and 08-08's recorder writes captured fixtures through this sanitizer.
Output: five new test Kotlin files and the manifest header.

Contention: wave 4 is this plan only. It starts after 08-03 has merged and never runs two Gradle invocations at once.
</objective>
