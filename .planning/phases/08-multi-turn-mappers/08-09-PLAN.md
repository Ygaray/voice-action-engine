<objective>
Record real, sanitized multi-turn conversations on Anthropic, OpenAI and OpenRouter as captured goldens (D-07, D-12). That closes the real halves of SC2 (a thinking golden with real signatures that survives byte-for-byte) and SC3 (golden tests built from recorded, sanitized real bodies), and turns the D-11 assumptions only a live answer can settle into findings.

The recorder, its ceilings and its key-free proof already exist (08-08). This plan only decides, runs, commits and reports.

The capture sits behind a `checkpoint:decision` routed through the master, as 05-12 did. The master already holds standing approval under Yahir's test-key policy, so the expected answer is approve, with defer as the fallback. Phases 1-8 are complete and green on derived fixtures without it.

Verifier note: if the gate resolves to defer, or a conversation is unmet, SC3 is met for Chat (real Phase 5 envelopes under derived conversations) and only partly met for Anthropic (no real Anthropic multi-turn body exists), and SC2's "the real API accepts our replayed signatures" half stays open. The SUMMARY must say so; it must not pass silently.

Contention: wave 9 is this plan only. It starts after 08-08 has merged and runs one Gradle invocation at a time.
</objective>
